Director Terms

Our amended and restated certificate of incorporation that will become effective immediately prior to the

completion of this offering will provide that, prior to the Class F Sunset Time, we will not have a classified board

of directors, and all directors will be elected for annual terms. Each directors term will continue until the next

annual meeting and until the election and qualification of their successor, or their earlier death, resignation, or

removal.

Following the Class F Sunset Time, our board of directors will shift to having a classified board of directors

consisting of three classes of approximately equal size, each serving staggered three-year terms. At such time,

Executive Summary

At Anthropic, we strive to build a team that is aligned with our mission and values. Executive compensation

reflects our overall compensation philosophy, which prioritizes competitive pay that targets the 90th percentile

of the AI development market, consistent pay for the same role, level, and location, and simplicity of plan

design. In 2025, we offered a competitive mix of base salary, long-term equity incentive awards (including stock

options and RSUs), and other benefits to attract and retain top talent in a competitive market environment.

Compensation Philosophy and Objectives

Our executive compensation philosophy is to provide our executive officers with consistent cash and equity

compensation for the same role, level, and location. When determining executive compensation, we review each

executives current compensation against role- and level-specific market data. We do this bi-annually as part of

our performance and compensation cycle where we review proposed market-based and promotion-based cash

and equity increases for executives. We plan to continue to regularly review, and potentially adjust, our

executive compensation programs to ensure they are consistent with our compensation philosophy as a public

company.

In determining compensation for our senior leaders, we distinguish between our seven co-founders (among

whom only Dr. Amodei and Ms. Amodei are executive officers) and our other senior leaders who are not co-

founders (among whom only Mr. Rao is an executive officer). Each co-founder is paid equal compensation. As a

non-co-founder senior leader, Mr. Rao received base salary and equity compensation based on market data for

his role and level.

Our executive compensation program is structured to achieve the objectives described below. We regularly

review our executive compensation program to assess whether it meets these objectives and adapts to evolving

economic conditions.

Competitive Pay to Attract and Retain Top Talent. We provide our executives with competitive cash

and equity pay for each role, location, and level to attract mission-aligned, top executive talent.

Internal Parity. We take internal parity into account when determining executive compensation. As

co-founders, Dr. Amodei and Ms. Amodei are awarded equal compensation, including any annual cash

or equity increases. As a non-co-founder senior leader, Mr. Rao is compensated based on the market

Compensation Recovery Policy

We have adopted an executive compensation recoupment policy intended to comply with the requirements of

Rule 10D-1 under the Exchange Act and the rules of the stock exchange on which our securities are listed

(Clawback Policy) that will be effective upon the effectiveness of the registration statement of which this

prospectus forms a part. The Clawback Policy will generally require us to recover erroneously awarded

incentive-based compensation that was received by covered executive officers in the event we are required to

prepare an accounting restatement due to any material non-compliance with any financial reporting

requirement under the securities laws (including any required accounting restatement to correct an error in

previously issued financial statements that is material to the previously issued financial statements), or that

would result in a material misstatement if the error were corrected in the current period or left uncorrected in

the current period. The Clawback Policy generally covers incentive-based compensation received on or after the

effective date of the Clawback Policy and during the three-year period prior to the date we are required to

prepare the financial restatement. The amount required to be recovered is the excess of the amount of

incentive-based compensation received over the amount that otherwise would have been received based on the

restated financial measure.
