The estimated fair value of the note purchase agreements was determined by using a probability-weighted

expected return method. The fair value of the Series G-2 redeemable convertible preferred stock was

determined by using a hybrid approach comprising a contingent claims analysis (CCA) and a common stock

equivalent (CSE) method. Assumptions included assumed future liquidity and financing events, the terms and

features of the agreement, weights applied to each scenario, discount rates, applicable risk free rates, the fair

value of the Series G-2 redeemable convertible preferred stock, and a discount for lack of marketability.

Level 3 liabilities consist of the SAFE liabilities (Note 9), convertible notes (Note 9), derivative liabilities (Note

9), donation matching program liability (Note 10), and a holdback liability for a portion of shares issuable in the

acquisition of Codeblog Corp. (Bun) (Note 15). There is no current market for these liabilities such that

determination of fair value requires significant judgment or estimation. Changes in fair value measurements

categorized within Level 3 of the fair value hierarchy are analyzed each period based on changes in estimates or

assumptions and recorded as appropriate. The Company recorded a $3 million change in fair value of the

holdback liability during the year ended December31, 2025 in other income (expense), net in the consolidated

statements of operations.

The SAFE liabilities are recorded at fair value, which is determined based on a third-party valuation utilizing a

scenario-based analysis and a CCA, that considers specific scenarios that factor in timing, probability, and

financing size. This model takes into account the contract terms as well as multiple inputs, such as the

Companys redeemable convertible preferred stock price, risk free interest rates, and expected volatility, some

of which the Company could not corroborate with observable market data. The assumptions used in valuing the

SAFE liability upon conversion on December 31, 2023 and on January 30, 2024, include volatility, risk free

interest rate, and term to liquidity. Changes in the fair value measurement of the SAFE liabilities relate to the

conversion scenarios and are recorded as change in fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities, and

SAFEs in the consolidated statements of operations. All SAFEs have been converted as of December31, 2024.

The estimated fair value of the convertible notes is determined by using a hybrid approach comprising a CCA

and a CSE method. The analysis estimates the fair value of the convertible notes based on the probability-

weighted expected future investment returns, considering possible outcomes available to the note holder,

including conversions to redeemable convertible preferred stock, Class N common stock, and maturity.

Assumptions include weight applied to the CCA and CSE methods, assumed volatility, time to a liquidity event,

need for and terms of financings or expected financings, and the risk free interest rate. Changes in fair value of

the convertible notes are recorded as change in fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities, and SAFEs

in the consolidated statements of operations.

Fair Value Option

The Company elected to account for the SAFEs and the convertible notes using the fair value option pursuant to

ASC 825 and changes in fair value related to the SAFEs and convertible notes are recorded in change in fair

value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities, and SAFEs in the Companys consolidated statements of

operations (Note 4). The Company elected to account for the convertible notes using the fair value option

pursuant to ASC 825 to eliminate the accounting complexity that would otherwise result from separately

accounting for the liability and equity components of the convertible notes under the traditional bifurcation

model.

The estimated fair value of the convertible notes, derivative liabilities, and SAFEs is determined by using a

scenario-based analysis that estimated the fair value of the convertible notes, derivative liabilities, and SAFEs

based on the probability-weighted present value of expected future investment returns, considering possible

outcomes available to the SAFE and convertible note holder, including conversions to redeemable convertible

preferred stock, Class N common stock and maturity. Issuance costs were expensed as incurred and are

reflected in other income (expense), net in the consolidated statements of operations. Changes in fair value of

the SAFEs and convertible notes and accrued interest are recorded as change in fair value of convertible notes,

derivative liabilities, and SAFEs in the consolidated statements of operations.

There were no changes in fair value attributable to credit risk recognized as of December 31, 2023, 2024, and

2025, as well as June 30, 2026 (unaudited).
