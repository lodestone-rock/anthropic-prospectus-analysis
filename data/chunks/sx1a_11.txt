Voting Rights

Class A common stock. Holders of our Class A common stock are entitled to one vote for each share

held on all matters submitted to a vote of stockholders generally, including the election of the directors

other than the Class T directors.

Class N common stock. Holders of our Class N common stock are not entitled to vote on any matter

that is submitted to a vote of stockholders, except as otherwise required by law, such as certain

amendments to our amended and restated certificate of incorporation that would adversely affect the

powers, preferences, or special rights of our Class N common stock without so adversely affecting our

Class A common stock, on which the DGCL entitles such holders to vote separately as a series.

Class T common stock. The holder of our Class T common stock has no voting rights with respect to

matters submitted to stockholders, except as set forth within our amended and restated certificate of

incorporation with respect to the election of a majority of our board of directors, the redemption of our

Class E common stock, certain amendments to the rights of our Class F common stock, or as otherwise

required by law, such as certain amendments to our amended and restated certificate of incorporation

that would adversely affect the powers, preferences, or special rights of our Class T common stock, on

which the DGCL entitles such holder to vote separately as a class.

Class F common stock. The holder of our Class F common stock will be entitled to an aggregate

number of votes that equals 50.1% of the total voting power of our outstanding shares of capital stock

as of the applicable record date on matters submitted to a vote of stockholders generally, including the

election of the directors other than the Class T directors. Our Class F common stock will be voted in the

manner provided by the Founder LLC Agreement. See the section titled Class F Common Stock

Voting Mechanics.

Conversion and Transferability

Class A common stock. Our Class A common stock will not be convertible into any other class or series

of capital stock. Subject to compliance with applicable law and the restrictions described in the section

titled Shares Eligible for Future SaleLock-Up and Market Standoff Agreements, our Class A

common stock can be transferred.

Class N common stock. The holders of our Class N common stock are primarily strategic business

partners. Subject to compliance with applicable law and the restrictions described in the section titled

Shares Eligible for Future SaleLock-Up and Market Standoff Agreements, our Class N common

stock can be transferred. The shares of Class N common stock will automatically convert into an equal

number of shares of Class A common stock upon a transfer effected through (i) a registered offering or

an open market sale of such shares or (ii) a transfer to a third party (A) that is not a holder of Class N

common stock (or an affiliate of a holder of Class N common stock) and (B) that will not beneficially

own or control more than 9.99% of the shares of any class or series of our equity securities after giving

effect to the transfer.

Class T common stock. Our Class T common stock will not be convertible into any other class or series

of capital stock. Our Class T common stock is transferable only upon the approval of persons

comprising the Trust Amendment Threshold. Because the persons comprising the Trust Amendment

Threshold have the power to modify or terminate the LTBT and the rights of our Class T common stock

entirely, there is no separate restriction on the identity of a permissible transferee of our Class T

common stock.

Class F common stock. Our Class F common stock will not be convertible into any other class or series

of capital stock. Our Class F common stock is not transferable except in the case of a reorganization or

redomiciliation of the Founder LLC that is approved by our board of directors and does not result in a

change to the members of the Founder LLC.

Class E common stock. Our Class E common stock will not be convertible into any other class or series

of capital stock and is not transferable.

Subject to preferences that may apply to any shares of preferred stock outstanding at the time, the holders of

our Class A common stock and Class N common stock are entitled to receive dividends out of funds legally

available if our board of directors, in its discretion, determines to issue dividends and then only at the times and

in the amounts that our board of directors may determine. Our Class T common stock, Class F common stock,

and Class E common stock are not entitled to receive dividends. See the section titled Dividend Policy for

more information.

No Preemptive or Similar Rights

provisions (other than those providing for the redemption of our Class T common stock, Class F common stock,

and Class E common stock for no consideration described above).

Multi-Class Common Stock

As described above in the section titled Common StockVoting Rights, our amended and restated

we expect that the LTBT and the Founder LLC will collectively have the ability to elect all members of our board

Classified Board

Our amended and restated certificate of incorporation that will become effective immediately prior to the

completion of this offering will provide that prior to the Class F Sunset Time, we will not have a classified board

of directors, and all directors will be elected for annual terms. Each directors term will continue until the next

annual meeting and until the election and qualification of their successor, or their earlier death, resignation, or

removal.

Following the Class F Sunset Time, our board of directors will shift to having a classified board of directors

consisting of three classes of approximately equal size, each serving staggered three-year terms. At such time,

our directors would be assigned by the then-current board of directors to a class. If our Class T common stock

remains outstanding, the Class T directors and the remaining directors shall be allocated among the classes so

that each class includes a proportionate number of Class T directors and other directors. Upon expiration of the

term of a class of directors, directors for that class would each be elected for a three-year term at the annual

meeting of stockholders in the year in which that term expires. As a result, only one class of directors would be

elected at each annual meeting of our stockholders, with the other classes continuing for the remainder of their

respective three-year terms. Each directors term would continue until the third annual meeting of stockholders

following their election and until their successor has been elected and qualified, or until their earlier death,

resignation, or removal. Any increase or decrease in the number of directors would be distributed among the

three classes so that, as nearly as practicable, each class would consist of one-third of our directors.

Rule 144

In general, Rule 144 provides that once we have been subject to the public company reporting requirements of

Section13 or Section15(d) of the Exchange Act for at least 90 days, a person who is not deemed to have been

one of our affiliates for purposes of the Securities Act at any time during the 90 days preceding a sale and who

has beneficially owned the shares of our Class A common stock proposed to be sold for at least six months is

entitled to sell those shares without complying with the manner of sale, volume limitation, or notice provisions

of Rule 144, subject to compliance with the public information requirements of Rule 144. If such a person has

beneficially owned the shares proposed to be sold for at least one year, including the holding period of any prior

owner other than our affiliates, then that person would be entitled to sell those shares without complying with

any of the requirements of Rule 144.

In general, Rule 144 provides that our affiliates or persons selling shares of our Class A common stock on behalf

of our affiliates are entitled to sell upon expiration of the lock-up and market standoff agreements described

above, within any three-month period, a number of shares of our Class A common stock that does not exceed

the greater of

1% of the number of shares of our Class A common stock then outstanding, which will equal

shares immediately after the completion of this offering or

the average weekly trading volume of our Class A common stock during the four calendar weeks

preceding the filing of a notice on Form 144 with respect to that sale.

Sales of our Class A common stock made in reliance upon Rule 144 by our affiliates or persons selling shares of

our Class A common stock on behalf of our affiliates are also subject to certain manner of sale provisions and

notice requirements and to the availability of current public information about us.

Rule 701

Rule 701 generally allows a stockholder who purchased shares of our capital stock pursuant to a written

compensatory plan or contract and who is not deemed to have been an affiliate of our company during the

immediately preceding 90 days to sell these shares in reliance upon Rule 144, but without being required to

comply with the public information, holding period, volume limitation, or notice provisions of Rule 144.

Rule701 also permits affiliates of our company to sell their Rule 701 shares under Rule 144 without complying

with the holding period requirements of Rule 144. All holders of Rule 701 shares, however, are required to wait

until 90 days after the date of this prospectus before selling those shares pursuant to Rule 701.

Backup Withholding and Information Reporting

Generally, we must report annually to the IRS the amount of dividends paid to you, your name and address,

and the amount of tax withheld, if any. A similar report will be sent to you. Pursuant to applicable income tax

treaties or other agreements, the IRS may make these reports available to tax authorities in your country of

Payments of dividends on or of proceeds from the sale or other disposition of our Class A common stock made

to you may be subject to information reporting and backup withholding (currently at a rate of 24%) unless you

establish an exemption, for example, by properly certifying your non-U.S. status on an IRS Form W-8BEN, IRS

Form W-8BEN-E, or another appropriate version of IRS Form W-8. Any documentation provided to an

applicable withholding agent may need to be updated in certain circumstances.

Information reporting and backup withholding will generally apply to the proceeds of a sale or other disposition

effected through a non-U.S. office of a broker with substantial U.S. ownership or operations will generally be

treated in a manner similar to sales or other dispositions effected through a U.S. office of a broker.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, backup withholding and information reporting may apply if either we or our

paying agent has actual knowledge, or reason to know, that you are a U.S. person. You should consult your own

tax advisors regarding the application of the information reporting and backup withholding rules in light of

your particular circumstances.

Backup withholding is not an additional tax. Rather, the U.S. federal income tax liability of persons subject to

backup withholding will be reduced by the amount of tax withheld. If withholding results in an overpayment of

taxes, a refund or credit may generally be obtained from the IRS, provided that the required information is

furnished to the IRS in a timely manner.
