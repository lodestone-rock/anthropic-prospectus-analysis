Overview

For the first time in history, intelligence is something humans can build. The cognitive capability to reason,

analyze, synthesize, decide, and create is producible at scale and accessible on demand. We believe this

abundance of AI will rapidly transform every sector of the global economy in ways greater than the impact of

industrialization, electricity, semiconductors, and the internet. AI that truly delivers on its potentialwith the

risks well managedcould significantly accelerate global growth and dramatically improve quality of life for

people around the world.

Anthropic was founded in 2021 by a team of researchers responsible for many of the breakthroughs that define

AI. We are first and foremost a research labour mission is the responsible development and maintenance of

advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity. This mission directs our focusalong with our talent and

culture, our frontier and safety research, our approach to compute, and our enterprise strategy, it is one of the

core advantages that set us apart. These advantages enable us to continuously advance frontier AI and shape

the standards for its safe and responsible development. Our frontier AI, Claude, is delivered to customers

through our models, platform, and applications. Claude has experienced the fastest enterprise adoption of any

technology in history, as demonstrated by revenue growing from $0 at commercial launch in the first quarter of

2023 to $17.0 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026. This rapid and growing adoption

generates two self-reinforcing dynamicsan improvement loop that advances Claude and an investment loop

that helps fund our continued research in frontier AI.

We believe the diffusion of AI through the global economy represents one of the largest opportunities in history

to improve how humans live and work, and this process is just beginning. In our view, the world has only begun

to use the capabilities of todays frontier models, and the gap between what Claude can do and how broadly it is

capabilities have evolved significantly in recent years, and we expect they will continue advancing rapidly. This

will lead not only to an expanding addressable market, but also to an expanding scope of impact on the world.

We aim to ensure that impact is as positive as possible.

Tom Brown) later co-founded Anthropic, was critical in establishing these relationships and helped guide the

wave of modern LLMs.

The Importance of Safety and Ethical Behavior

While the potential benefits of AI are extraordinary, its rapid advancement will impact the economy and society

in ways that could create significant disruption, particularly if the technology is not developed and deployed

responsibly. For example, bad actors have already conducted high-profile cyberattacks using AIs capabilities to

discover vulnerabilities, generate malware, and evade detection tools other risks associated with misuse,

including in the domain of biology, could result in even greater harm. AI deployed at scale also has the potential

to concentrate power and wealth both within and between countries in ways that could erode trust, harm

society, and destabilize the geopolitical order. Misaligned AI systems could corrupt institutional decision-

making or cause large-scale harm through autonomous actions that are not visible or understandable to their

users and operators. We believe that frontier AI companies must invest in safety and alignment to help ensure

the responsible diffusion of this technology. We also believe that safety, alignment, and ethical behavior

resonate particularly strongly with enterprises, where rigorous safeguards, predictable model behavior, and

demonstrable accountability have become decisive criteria in selecting an AI partner for mission-critical

workloads.

Platform Features

Platform features enable developers to customize their usage of Claude, including

Extended Thinking. Claude can perform additional internal reasoning before producing a final

response, working through complex, multi-step problems to improve response quality on tasks like

analysis, math, and coding.

Adaptive Thinking. Claude automatically calibrates whether a given request calls for extended

reasoning and how much reasoning to applyso developers get the benefits of extended reasoning

without configuring reasoning levels request by request.

Effort Controls. Developers can specify an effort level on each request to guide how much reasoning

Claude applies, allowing them to balance response quality against cost and latency.

Tool Use. Claude can natively call external functions and APIs throughout a conversation, with support

for parallel execution and interleaved thinking between tool calls. The search tool loads only the tools

required for a given task on demand, which can reduce token overhead compared to loading full tool

libraries upfront.

Long Context. Claude supports large context windows and maintains its strong performance even as

inputs grow, meaning that developers can include entire codebases, large document sets, or long agent

histories in a single request.

Structured Outputs. Developers can constrain Claudes responses to a specified schema, such as valid

JSON (JavaScript Object Notation), so results integrate reliably with downstream systems and

application code with less additional parsing or validation.

Code Execution. A sandboxed execution environment where Claude can write, run, and iterate on code

within an agent loop, which is foundational for data analysis and automation workflows. This

environment lets Claude call tools and process large volumes of data more efficiently and run the

scripts packaged in Agent Skills.

Web Search. A native tool giving Claude access to real-time web information, enabling grounded,

citation-aware responses in production applications.

Files API. Allows developers to upload documents once and reference them across multiple sessions,

simplifying workflows that depend on persistent documents.

Context Management

Harnesses

A model alone is not an agent. Running one reliably in production is challenging, because agents must survive

crashes and interruptions, act on systems with scoped and auditable credentials, and scale across variable

workloads. Autonomous operation requires a harness, which provides the secure environment, credentials,

memory, and orchestration loop a model needs to plan, call tools, verify its work, and complete a task safely on

its own. Building and operating that infrastructure is specialized, continuous work that, until recently, each

customer had to take on themselves. We offer two ways to run a harness on the Claude Platform

Agent SDK. This solution makes the same production-grade harness that powers Claude Code available

to all developers, to run on their own compute. It provides built-in context management, session

handling, multi-agent coordination, and prompt optimization, so customers can build and run

sophisticated agents on their own infrastructure in a fraction of the time otherwise required.

Claude Managed Agents. An out-of-the-box solution in which we operate the harness on our

customers behalf, on Anthropic-managed compute. We host the sandboxes, store the credentials,

maintain the memory, and run the agent loop customers define their agents tasks, tools, and

guardrails. When a new model becomes available, customers do not need to rebuild their agent loop.

Our Data Center Strategy

Historically, we purchased compute under fixed-time agreements. As our compute requirements have scaled,

we have transitioned to a hybrid model in which, in addition to purchasing compute as a cloud service, we also

procure specialized AI accelerators directly and operate this hardware in dedicated data center facilities leased

and operated on our behalf, to our specification. We refer to this additional source of compute as Anthropic

Direct. This transition is driven in part by the scale of our compute requirements as well as the potential long-

term economic advantages of direct infrastructure leasing and hosting. Our first data center facility became

operational this year.

Anthropic Direct also lets us separate our commitment to data center capacity from our commitment to the AI

accelerators deployed inside it. Because data centers require longer lead times but represent a smaller fraction

of total compute costs, this approach enables us to secure data center capacity early while deferring larger

accelerator investments until our specific compute needs are more certain.

Alignment

Interpretability

Unlike traditional software, where unwanted outputs are traceable to specific bugs, the inner workings of LLMs

like Claude are more inscrutable. Our researchers describe AI models as grown, not builtsoftware

developers can set the initial conditions and some constraints, but they cannot fully control any given output or

behavior. This makes assurances about safetyand the diagnosis of problemsfar more challenging for AI than

for standard software. Anthropic is widely recognized as a leader in mechanistic interpretability the science of

how LLMs work. We have developed mathematical techniques to map how models form and connect concepts

as they reason through problems investigated how they reflect constructs analogous to psychological states and

thought processes and tested how their behavior can be steered through direct adjustments to internal

representations. We use interpretability research along with other alignment research techniques to test for

unwanted behavioral tendencies before a model release.

Safeguards

Our Safeguards team reduces the risk of Claude producing dangerous, harmful, or undesirable content through

multiple approaches, including probes and classifiers that monitor inputs and outputs, training against specific

detailed analysis to determine whether new models provide meaningful new capabilities to assist potential

attackers, and our Safeguards team maintains dedicated classifiers and monitoring systems targeting cyber-

We make decisions about model development and deployment, including whether to begin training a new

model, on a case-by-case basis. Before releasing a frontier model, we consider a variety of factors, such as the

capabilities and behaviors of the specific model at issue and of our models overall, how well our safeguards and

security measures work, and how widely the model would be available. We plan to extend our testing to include

Our Governance Structure and Its Goals

Our novel capital and corporate governance structure has been carefully designed and differs significantly from

that of other public companies. We strive to help bring humanity safely into an era where transformative AI

exerts tremendous and potentially decisive influence over a broad range of human undertakings. Operating as a

PBC gives our board of directors the necessary latitude to balance the pecuniary interests of our stockholders,

the best interests of those materially affected by our conduct, and the Company Purpose. In addition, a

financially disinterested LTBT, which generally has the power to elect and remove a majority of the authorized

members of our board of directors, is designed to supply both the incentives and insights for striking this

balance appropriately. We also believe that our seven co-founders are distinctly equipped to be stewards of our

mission.

Public Benefit Corporation

We are incorporated as a PBC under Delaware law, a structure that enables our commitment to AI safety and

responsibility. PBCs are a class of corporations that are intended to produce a public benefit and to operate in a

responsible and sustainable manner. As a PBC, we are required to balance (i) the pecuniary interests of our

stockholders, (ii) the best interests of those materially affected by our conduct, and (iii) the Company Purpose.

This requires our board of directors to weigh multiple factors, including relevant long- and short-term

externalities of decisions, alongside the pecuniary interests of our stockholders. To date, we have found that the

safety and societal benefit of AI and consideration of the best interests of all key stakeholder groups have

generally aligned with our long-term commercial success and stockholders long-term monetary interests.

However, as a PBC, we may make decisions that we believe appropriately balance the pecuniary interests of our

stockholders, the best interests of those materially affected by our conduct, and the Company Purpose, but that

negatively impact the short-, medium-, or long-term performance of our business or the market price of our

Class A common stock. There is limited judicial precedent interpreting the balancing requirement applicable to

PBCs under Delaware law, and there is therefore uncertainty as to how a court would view our directors

balancing of these potentially competing interests. For a discussion of these risks, see the section titled Risk

FactorsOur focus on the long-term best interests of our company as a PBC, and the duty of our directors to

balance the pecuniary interests of our stockholders, the best interests of those stakeholders materially affected

by our conduct, and the Company Purpose, may conflict with short-, medium-, or long-term financial interests

and business performance, which may negatively impact the value of our Class A common stock and Risk

In measuring our public benefit performance, our board of directors will consider the objectives set forth in our

Long-Term Benefit Trust

We created the LTBT, which was designed to ensure that we are managed in a manner that responsibly

balances our tripartite goals as a PBC (i) the pecuniary interests of our stockholders, (ii) the best interests of

those materially affected by our conduct, and (iii) the Company Purpose. The LTBT is organized as a common

law noncharitable purpose trust under Delaware law and governed by the Long-Term Benefit Trust Amended

and Restated Trust Agreement (the Trust Agreement). The LTBT is intended to operate as an independent

body of Trustees with expertise in AI safety, national security, public policy, and social enterprise. The LTBTs

arrangements are designed to minimize the LTBTs and the Trustees exposure to our financial performance

and grant the Trustees sufficient independence to ensure we are striking this balance appropriately.

Through a special class of common stock, Class T common stock, the LTBT generally has the authority to elect

and remove the Class T directors, who together will constitute a majority of the authorized members of our

board of directors. Class T common stock has no economic rights.

Decisions regarding our business and affairs are made by our board of directors. While the LTBT has significant

influence over our board of directors through its ability to generally elect and remove Class T directors, neither

the LTBT nor the Trustees have decision-making authority in the day-to-day management or strategy setting of

the company. The LTBTs role is to select directors who are well equipped to responsibly balance our tripartite

goals as a PBC, and to support our managements decision-making through advice and guidance. Certain

actions, such as a merger, consolidation, or sale of all or substantially all of our assets, will require the approval

of our board of directors pursuant to Delaware law, a majority of whom are elected by the LTBT.
