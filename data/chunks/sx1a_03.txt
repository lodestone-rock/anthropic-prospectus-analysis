Electrical Power and Fuel Constraints

The operation of data centers at the scale required for frontier AI development and deployment consumes

substantial quantities of electrical power. The availability of sufficient electrical power has emerged as a

significant constraint on data center development and expansion in many geographies, including the United

States. Electric grid infrastructure in the United States and other countries was not designed to accommodate

the rapid increase in demand associated with AI development, and the lead times required to build new

generation capacity, transmission infrastructure, and grid interconnections can extend for years. Demand on

electric grid infrastructure is also increasing as a result of the increased electrification of homes, vehicles, and

industry, and this growing demand is occurring at a time when power generation capacity additions,

transmission expansion, and grid modernization efforts have not kept pace and certain existing generation

resources are being retired, all of which has contributed to higher electricity prices and longer interconnection

timelines in certain regions. We have publicly committed to covering the cost of electricity price increases to

consumers caused by our data centers, including both the cost of electricity itself and the infrastructure

necessary to deliver it.

delay pre-training a new frontier base model for more than a month to allow time to further enhance our

If we are unable to attract, retain, or motivate highly skilled personnel, we may not be able to

grow or operate effectively.

Our performance and ability to grow our business depend on the talents and efforts of highly skilled

individuals, and our future success depends on our continuing ability to identify, hire, develop, motivate, and

retain highly skilled personnel for all areas of our organization. Our business is particularly dependent on a

limited pool of researchers, engineers, and technical personnel with specialized experience in designing,

training, deploying, and operating frontier models and the underlying infrastructure that supports them. We

have from time to time experienced, and we expect to continue to experience, difficulty in hiring and retaining

highly skilled employees with appropriate qualifications at the pace that our operations require. Competition in

our industry relating to hiring and retaining employees with appropriate qualifications and on competitive

economic terms is exceptionally intense and has escalated significantly in recent periods, especially in the San

Francisco Bay Area, where our headquarters is located and where we have a substantial presence and need for

highly skilled personnel. A small number of well-capitalized AI labs, large technology companies, and new

market entrants compete aggressively for a relatively fixed supply of individuals with the requisite expertise.

Certain of our competitors have directly solicited, and continue to directly solicit, our employees. Our former

employees are also generally able to join or found competing companies immediately following their departure,

and, subject to their confidentiality and other legal obligations, use the general skills, know-how, and

experience they developed while employed by us. In addition, we have in the past, and may in the future, be

returns on these investments that are commensurate with the level of our expenditures. If the perceived value

including our co-founders, who hold equity in our company or whose equity awards are or will become

wealth. As a result, it may be difficult for us to continue to retain and motivate these employees, and the value

of their holdings could affect their decisions about whether or not they continue to work for us.

Historically, our ability to successfully attract, retain, and motivate our personnel has in large part been due to

our company culture and commitment to safety and responsible scaling and development. If we are unable to

maintain our culture, if we make decisions as a company that our employees do not believe align with our

values, or if events take place, regardless of fault, that call into question whether we have operated in

accordance with our values, our ability to attract, retain, and motivate our personnel may be adversely affected.

to accurately forecast headcount needs, periods of accelerated hiring followed by corrective actions that may
