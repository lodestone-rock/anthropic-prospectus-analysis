Certain estimates in this prospectus regarding global knowledge workers wages, which we refer to as the

annual economic value represented by global knowledge workers, are our own, derived from third-party data.

Based on wage data for U.S. workers by occupation classification from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics in

2025, we calculate the total salary for the subset of workers that we believe to represent knowledge workers.

Based on International Labor Organization data for global labor income as a percentage of global GDP, and

based on 2025 global GDP, we estimate a 2025 global wages figure. We calculate the ratio of total U.S. wages in

2025 to the 2025 global wages figure, and apply that ratio to the total U.S. knowledge workers wages figure to

arrive at an estimate of global knowledge workers wages.
